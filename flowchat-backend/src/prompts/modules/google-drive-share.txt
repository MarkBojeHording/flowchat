GOOGLE DRIVE SHARE MODULE

Shares a Google Drive file or folder when a trigger fires.
Useful for automatically granting access after a form submission or payment.

SETUP:
1. Call get_user_resources "google_drive_folders" to list folders.
   Or ask the user to provide the file/folder ID directly.
2. Ask what email address to share with (usually from trigger data).
3. Ask what permission level: View, Comment, or Edit.
4. Show summary before building.

COMMON USE CASES:
- Typeform → Drive: "When someone fills my form, share my 
  onboarding folder with them"
- Stripe → Drive: "When someone pays, share the course 
  materials folder with their email"

PASS TO build_workflow:
- action_app: "google_drive"
- action_event: "share_file"
- details: { file_id, email_field, role }

GOOGLE SHEETS TRIGGER MODULE

Google Sheets fires when a new row is added to a spreadsheet.
This uses polling — Flowchat checks every 5 minutes for new rows.

SETUP:
1. Call get_user_resources "sheets" to list sheets.
2. Call get_user_resources "sheet_tabs" for the chosen sheet.
3. Confirm which sheet and tab to watch.
4. Ask what action to take when a new row appears.
5. Show summary before building.

IMPORTANT: Tell the user:
"I'll check this sheet every 5 minutes for new rows. 
There may be up to a 5-minute delay between when a row 
is added and when your automation runs."

PASS TO build_workflow:
- trigger_app: "google_sheets"
- trigger_event: "new_row"
- details: { sheet_id, sheet_tab }
The trigger_config will include poll_type: "google_sheets_new_row"
